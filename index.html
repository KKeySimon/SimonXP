Simon Kye

Hi! I’m Simon — a computer scientist who studied at Boston University completing the BA/MS dual degree in Computer Science. I’m Korean, grew up in Hong Kong, and now live in the United States, which has helped me adapt quickly across different cultures and environments.

I love programming because when something goes wrong, it’s never for some mystical reason — there’s always a logical sequence of steps behind how code behaves. At the same time, software engineering leaves room for huge creative liberty in how you design and build things.

What motivates me most is seeing real-world impact from something I started doing for fun as a kid — from making criminal justice records more accessible to helping identify missing children with machine learning.
